# Free is genuinely free — forever. It runs on your machine, so it costs us nothing to provide, at any scale. Pro is the hosted service that watches your agents continuously; that's what you pay for.

## faq

Does the free tool send my data anywhere? No. The web app and CLI analyze on your device — see privacy. Pro is opt-in and processes only what you connect.

How is Pro billed? Per active developer — only the devs who open a pull request that month. No PR, no charge. Unlimited repos. (This is the CodeRabbit model — it grows with your team, not your repo sprawl.)

Can I cancel anytime? Yes — it's a month-to-month subscription, cancel from your billing portal.

I just want the audit, not the cloud. Great — the free web/CLI does that fully, keep using it.
