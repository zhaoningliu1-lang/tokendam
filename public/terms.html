The service

TokenDam is a free tool that analyzes an LLM request "trace" you provide and reports where tokens may be wasted, with suggested fixes. It runs in your browser or as a local CLI. Provided "as is."

How the numbers work (and their limits)

  • Token counts use the OpenAI o200k tokenizer as a cross-vendor approximation — typically within ~10–15% for Claude / DeepSeek / Gemini, and flagged as an estimate in the report.
  • Prices come from a small, manually-maintained table and may be out of date; providers change pricing frequently.
  • "Potential savings," per-call, and $/month figures are illustrative projections based on a single trace, not a measurement of your actual bill or a guarantee that any fix will save money.
  • Suggested fixes (including model-downgrade suggestions) are judgment calls that may affect output quality; test before applying.
